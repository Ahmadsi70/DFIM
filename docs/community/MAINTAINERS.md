## Maintainer Responsibilities

- Review and merge pull requests within 5 business days
- Triage security reports within 24 hours (critical) or 5 days (other)
- Participate in monthly maintainer sync calls
- Vote on governance changes and major design decisions

## Becoming a Maintainer

Contributors who demonstrate:
- 5+ merged, non-trivial pull requests
- Active participation in code review and issue triage
- Adherence to the Code of Conduct
- Understanding of DFIM's architecture and security model

may be nominated by any existing maintainer. Nomination requires a
supermajority vote (>66%) of active maintainers for approval.

## Emeritus Maintainers

Maintainers who are inactive for 6+ months may be moved to emeritus status.
Emeritus maintainers retain their title but lose voting rights. They may
return to active status by request.
